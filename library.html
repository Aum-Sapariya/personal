Who Moved My Cheese?
Business · Change

Who Moved My Cheese?

by Spencer Johnson

A simple parable about two mice and two little people who react differently when their cheese supply runs out. It explores how to deal with change gracefully in work and life.

The Alchemist
Fiction · Quest

The Alchemist

by Paulo Coelho

A magical tale of Santiago, a young Andalusian shepherd who journeys to the Egyptian desert in search of a worldly treasure, only to discover the profound treasures hidden within his own heart.

Rich Dad Poor Dad
Finance · Mindset

Rich Dad Poor Dad

by Robert Kiyosaki

A classic that contrasts the mindsets of two father figures—one highly educated but financially struggling, and the other a school dropout who became a wealthy entrepreneur. It reshapes how we view assets and liabilities.

Ikigai
Philosophy · Lifestyle

Ikigai

by Héctor García & Francesc Miralles

An exploration of the Japanese concept of 'ikigai'—a reason for being. It uncovers the secrets to a long, happy life by finding the intersection of what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.

Eat That Frog
Productivity · Time Mgmt

Eat That Frog

by Brian Tracy

A practical guide to overcoming procrastination. Tracy argues that if you 'eat the frog'—tackle your hardest, most important task first thing in the morning—the rest of the day will feel remarkably easy.

The Prophet
Philosophy · Poetry

The Prophet

by Kahlil Gibran

A beautiful collection of poetic essays where Almustafa, the chosen prophet, shares his timeless wisdom on joy, sorrow, work, and love with the people of Orphalese before his departure.

Red Rising Trilogy
Sci-Fi · Dystopia

Red Rising Trilogy

by Pierce Brown

A thrilling sci-fi epic following Darrow, a Red laborer in a color-coded society on Mars. Driven by tragedy, he infiltrates the elite Golds to tear down the oppressive hierarchy from within.

Psychology of Money
Finance · Behavior

Psychology of Money

by Morgan Housel

Housel explores how behavior, not math, dictates financial success. The book delves into how making good financial decisions is less about what you know and more about how you behave.

The Iliad
Epic · Mythology

The Iliad

by Homer

An ancient Greek epic poem set during the final weeks of the ten-year Trojan War. It explores the rage of Achilles, the wrath of the gods, and the tragic, fleeting nature of heroism and mortality.
