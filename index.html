01.
Bygone — things that have passed

The road behind,
written in small hours
and hard light.

The story begins on a quiet Friday — the 23rd of February, 2007. From that first breath, the road behind has been a slow accumulation of small hours, hard lessons, and gentle realizations, shaping the quiet rhythm of who I am becoming.

2007

The Beginning

2010

The First Shift

Early Childhood · Porbandar

For the first three years, life was a quiet, unblemished happiness. But seasons change, and sometimes the ground shifts beneath us without warning. Due to unforeseen circumstances, we had to leave the warmth of our village behind. The road led us to Porbandar city, where the next three years gently unfolded. It was there I first stepped into the world outside, completing my preschool and first standard—a bittersweet beginning in a new place, carrying the quiet echoes of the home we had left behind.

Porbandar · Where the sea meets memory
2013

The Transit Year

Childhood · Dhoraji

After three years by the sea, the winds shifted again. We packed our quiet echoes and moved to Dhoraji city. It was a brief chapter—just one year—but a necessary pause between the place we had grown into and the city that would eventually become our home.

2014

The Golden Years

Childhood · Rajkot

Then came the move to Rajkot. We rented a house, and for the next four years, I lived what felt like the best days of my childhood. I made friends so wholesome that their memories still echo in quiet corners of my mind. We celebrated countless festivals together, laughed without a care, and ran through those streets without ever keeping track of time. I didn't even notice where those four years went.

2018

A Quiet Restart

Early Teens · Rajkot

Eventually, we moved again—this time, just across the city to our own home. There was a quiet joy in finally having a place of our own, but a deep, aching sadness in leaving the streets and the friends that had become my whole world. Yet, a new home brings a quiet restart. I made new friends, met new neighbors, and built almost a new life from scratch.

Rajkot · A place of our own
2019

The Great Pause & The Search

The Pandemic Years · Lockdown

Then came 2019, and with it, the pandemic. The world shut its doors, and life as we knew it changed overnight. Confined to the walls of our home, I began to explore. I kept throwing things at the wall to see what would stick—trying my hand at coding, sketching art, writing poems, and losing myself in books, games, shows, and movies. It was a season of quiet isolation, but also of restless searching, trying to find my own niche in the sudden stillness.

2022

The Return to the Light

Post-Pandemic · High School

Slowly, the world began to breathe again. The lockdowns lifted, the streets filled back up, and life found its way back to normal. I stepped into the 10th grade, trading the quiet isolation of my room for the noise of the classroom once more. The transition was strange at first, but the rhythm of life quickly caught up, pulling me forward.

2023

The Crossroads

10th Grade · Rajkot

In 2023, I completed my 10th grade. It was the first major crossroads—the question of what to become. After the restless searching of the lockdown years, I found my direction in the logic of numbers. I chose the commerce field and set my sights on becoming a Chartered Accountant. It was a decision made not in haste, but with a quiet certainty that this was the path I wanted to walk.

2025

A New Threshold

12th Grade · Commerce

The next two years were a blur of ledgers, tax codes, and late-night studying. In 2025, I completed my 12th grade. The foundation has been laid, the first major step taken. Now, the real crucible begins.

The present is a narrow window —
everything I am is happening
inside this thin sliver of light.

I officially embarked on this path on the 18th of March, 2025. Months of quiet labor culminated on the 3rd of November, 2025, when I passed my CA Foundation. Yet, beneath the steady rhythm of these days, one question haunts the quiet hours: will I pass CA Intermediate on my first attempt?
