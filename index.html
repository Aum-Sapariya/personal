Currently, I am wading through the dense volumes of CA Intermediate—navigating advanced accounting, taxation laws, and audit frameworks. The days blend into nights, but the logic of numbers keeps the mind sharp.

Reading

The Prophet by Kahlil Gibran

Currently reading this profound collection of poetic essays. It is a book that teaches the quiet dignity of labor. It holds a mirror to the soul, reminding me that "when you work you are a flute through whose heart the whispering of the hours turns into music." It reshapes how I view the daily grind.

Watching

Game of Thrones

Currently rewatching this epic saga. I am captivated by its political intricacies and the deep, unflinching exploration of power. It is a masterclass in how actions ripple outward, affecting the loyalty, emotions, and mentality of everyone in their wake. There are no pure heroes or villains, only complex choices and consequences.

Anime

One Piece

Deep into this vast, adventurous ocean. I am drawn to its profound willingness to tackle real-world shadows like corruption, bribery, and slavery. It proves that animation can carry the weight of our heaviest truths, blending profound tragedy with unyielding hope and camaraderie.

Playing

Chess

Moving pieces, thinking three steps ahead, and enjoying the quiet tension of the board. It is a mental gymnasium where calculation meets intuition. Every opening is a conversation, every mid-game a battle of resources, and the endgame a quiet, inevitable truth. It mirrors the strategic patience required in my studies.
